FAQ

What is AETHRIZ?

An AI-powered health intelligence platform that analyzes health data and provides personalized diet, lifestyle and health insights based on your unique profile.

What health data can AETHRIZ analyze?

Laboratory reports, health parameters, dietary habits, lifestyle information and other available health data — combined into one personalized health profile.

How is AETHRIZ different from normal health apps?

Most apps give general advice. AETHRIZ connects your health data with your lifestyle and diet to generate recommendations specifically for you.

Does AETHRIZ replace a doctor?

No. AETHRIZ supports health awareness and informed decisions. It does not replace qualified medical professionals, diagnosis or emergency care.

Does it work for different diets, languages, ages and lifestyles?

Yes. Its intelligence layer adapts recommendations to your health data, dietary preferences, lifestyle, language and context.
